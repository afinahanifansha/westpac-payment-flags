# Demo and recording guide

1. Open the prototype and select Maya. Show her boutique dress purchase, the clothing-spend flag, bill impact and the available choices.
2. Select Trusted person. George pairs with David through the simulated QR/code invitation. Advance to the roofing payment. David receives a request, gives an opinion, and George decides. Personal discussions occur outside the app.
3. Select Possible fraud. Sam is in Paris while the fictional card transaction is in Perth. Open the notification, review the mismatch and demonstrate the protection or recognition branch.
4. If time permits, show Smart SafeBlock and Flag settings as additional concept exploration.

Use Escape for a clean recording view and R to restart. Keys 1–5 work while the panel is hidden. Keep both simulated phones visible for George and David. Adjust browser window/zoom as needed.

Clearly identify synthetic data, scripted AI explanations and simulated banking actions in the presentation. The supplementary Smart SafeBlock scenario is a proposed extension.

Suggested 10-minute allocation: opening 1 minute; scenario demo 3 minutes; technical explanation 5 minutes; closing 1 minute. Rehearse to finish below the limit.
