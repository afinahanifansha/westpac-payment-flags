## Idea summary

Payment Flags is a proposed AI-powered review experience that helps customers understand unusual transactions, their estimated impact on bills and savings, and their available next steps. An optional trusted person can offer a second opinion on a specific payment, while the customer retains the decision within bank security controls.

The demo follows Maya shopping at a boutique, George considering a payment to an unfamiliar roofer with David’s advice, and Sam in Paris receiving an alert about a card transaction in Perth.

The intended value is timely, personally relevant guidance that connects risk signals with clear explanations and customer choices. Proposed AI handles contextual pattern recognition, forecasting and evidence-grounded explanations; fixed rules handle thresholds and permissions, and arithmetic handles financial calculations.

The submitted software is a static interaction prototype with fictional data, scripted explanations and simulated banking actions. It does not include a live AI model or real Westpac integration.
